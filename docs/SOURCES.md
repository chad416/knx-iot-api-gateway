# Sources

read on 16 November 2026. Paywalled standard text is not quoted; where a standard is named, the
accessible document that was actually read is listed.

## Not found or not verified

- No public measurement of how often a KNX device misses a frame that another group member
  acknowledged, and none of how often people press wall switches. Both are assumptions with a
  sensitivity run (DOSSIER section 7).
- Reaction time of a switch actuator between frame and relay: no data sheet value was looked up;
  8 to 20 ms is an assumption with a sensitivity run.
- The transfer time of a frame from the host processor to a TP-UART type transceiver is not
  modelled and was not looked up.
- The rule "the gateway may wait 3 bit times longer than the minimum" rests on S2 describing the
  idle times as waiting times to be observed. I did not find a sentence that explicitly allows or
  forbids a longer wait, and I have not tried it on hardware.
